Privacy Policy

Perioperative Antithrombotic Decision-Support Tool  |  Last updated: August 2026

Summary: This application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or patient information. Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The app has no backend server, no database, no analytics, and no account system. Any values you enter (such as creatinine clearance, medications, or procedure details) are processed entirely within your device's browser or app session and are discarded when you close or reset the tool. None of this information is transmitted over the internet, logged, or retained by the developer.

2. No Tracking or Advertising

The app contains no tracking technologies, no third-party advertising, no cookies used for tracking, and no fingerprinting. We do not build user profiles or monitor usage.

3. No Protected Health Information (PHI)

The tool is intended for educational and decision-support purposes and is designed so that no patient-identifying information is required or stored. Users should not enter patient-identifying information. Because nothing is transmitted or saved off-device, the app does not create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI.

4. Local Storage

The app may store a single non-personal preference on your device (for example, whether you dismissed the "Add to Home Screen" prompt). This never leaves your device and contains no personal or clinical data.

5. Medical Disclaimer

This tool provides advisory, educational information based on published clinical guidelines. It is not a medical device and does not replace independent clinical judgment. All recommendations must be verified by a qualified clinician against current full-text guidelines and the individual patient before any clinical action.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for use by healthcare professionals and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
